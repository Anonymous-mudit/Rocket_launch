Nine minutes from the pad to orbit.

What you just scrolled through follows a real two-stage flight profile. Every number on the telemetry readout tracks the moment it represents.

  1. T−00:00:03

    Ignition

    Nine engines light in sequence. Clamps hold the vehicle down until the computers confirm full thrust.

  2. T+00:01:10

    Max-Q

    The rocket passes through peak aerodynamic stress as it goes supersonic, so the engines briefly throttle down.

  3. T+00:02:42

    Stage separation

    The booster cuts off and falls away. The upper stage continues alone in near-vacuum, where its exhaust plume balloons outward.

  4. T+00:08:56

    Orbit

    Second-stage cutoff at orbital speed. The fairing halves are gone and the satellite unfolds its solar arrays.
